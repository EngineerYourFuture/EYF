# EYF Innovation Roadmap

The grounded, risk-calibrated growth strategy (endorsed 2026-07-20). Deliberately
excludes the aggressive bets (ISA / "free-until-placed", talent warranty, replacing
campus placements, becoming the standard score) — those carry capital, regulatory
(Indian placement-guarantee law), or liability risk and are revisited only later, from
strength, with proof and capital. Everything below is high-leverage and low-risk.

The through-line of every item is the same moat: **the verified skill → outcome graph.**

---

## Track A — More users (cheap, low-risk acquisition)

### A1. TPO / College Placement-Cell channel  `[flagship · net-new]`
The TPO (Training & Placement Officer) is the gatekeeper for a whole campus. Give them a
dashboard of their batch's aggregate readiness/health and company-fit; win one TPO,
onboard a batch. B2B2C, colleges pay, zero capital/regulatory exposure. Cheapest bulk
acquisition in the Indian market. **Exists:** nothing (org system models companies, not
colleges; students carry a free-text `college`). **v1 (this pass):** batch-analytics engine
+ admin-gated view (EYF targets strong colleges to pitch). **v2:** TPO self-serve accounts;
readiness-based batch stats (needs HARD-6 materialized readiness).

### A2. Warm alumni referral network  `[net-new, extends A: referral engine done]`
Placed alumni refer juniors into their own companies, with the junior's verified readiness
attached. Warm referral is the #1 real-world hiring channel; only EYF has the placement
graph + the proof. Builds on the shipped referral engine.

### A3. Vernacular / Bharat  `[execution, not a risky bet]`
Hindi/Tamil/Telugu/Kannada + tier-2/3 content. 10x's the addressable base. Proven playbook
(Byju's/Unacademy). Large content effort; sequence after A1 proves the channel.

## Track B — More subscriptions (product depth that justifies Pro)

### B1. Prescriptive "Path to Offer"  `[extend, not rebuild]`
**Exists:** `/offer` predicts the salary package + names "the one lever"; readiness coach
names "biggest lever." **Net-new:** the ranked *minimal action set* (top 3–5 moves) to a
target offer with each move's projected impact. Extend the existing offer/guidance surface.

### B2. Interview Autopsy  `[extend weakness-review]`
Cross-mock pattern analysis ("you freeze on system-design scaling; you talk too fast under
pressure") → targeted drill. **Exists:** `weakness-review` component (single-view). Net-new:
the cross-mock compounding analysis.

### B3. Parent progress digest  `[DONE 2026-07-20]`
Weekly WhatsApp/email to the parent (the payer + the anxious party in India): "Rahul —
12-day streak, readiness +6, on track." Drives conversion + retention. Email/push infra
exists. Self-contained; a strong early build.

### B4. Auto-Apply agent  `[net-new, larger]`
Agent auto-applies to matching roles, tailors the resume per role (ATS exists), files into
the pipeline. Magic moment. Larger scope (external application surfaces).

## Track C — More profit (employer-paid, the safe version)

### C1. Placement success fees  `[DONE 2026-07-20]`
Standard recruiting model: employer pays a fee (8–15% of first-year salary) when a vetted
candidate is hired through EYF. Proven, no warranty/ISA liability. Employer revenue dwarfs
B2C subs. **Exists:** org-hire pipeline (requisitions/candidates/offers) — the fee/billing
layer on top is net-new.

---

## Sequencing (don't do it all at once)

1. **A1 (TPO analytics)** — the growth engine; start here (this pass builds the core).
2. **B3 (parent digest)** + **B1 (path-to-offer)** — cheap subscription levers.
3. **A2 (warm referrals)** — extends the shipped referral engine.
4. **C1 (success fees)** — turn the existing hire pipeline into employer revenue.
5. **A3 (vernacular)** + **B4 (auto-apply)** — bigger execution bets once the above prove out.

Each item makes the skill→outcome graph bigger; a bigger graph makes every later bet more
defensible. That compounding is the moat.
