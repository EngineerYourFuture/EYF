# Compliance, Legal & Privacy

**Section 21 of PRD.**

---

## Pre-Launch Requirements

| Item | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Terms of Service | Required before launch |
| Privacy Policy | Required before launch |
| Consent Capture | Version + timestamp + IP hash captured on acceptance |

---

## Data Rights

| Right | Implementation |
|-------|---------------|
| Export | User can export their data |
| Correct | User can update their profile/data |
| Delete | User can request account deletion |

---

## Data Retention

- Define retention windows per data class (e.g., logs, submissions, billing events)
- Billing events: retain per legal/tax requirements
- Audit logs: long-term retention (immutable)

---

## Regional Considerations

| Area | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Minors policy | Age verification / guardian consent where required by region |
| Regional consent | Cookie and analytics consent behaviour varies by region (e.g., GDPR for EU) |

---

## Cookie / Analytics Consent

- Consent management required by region
- No analytics events fired without consent where required
